How the schedule works

Every card sits in a box. Recall it and it moves up: it returns after 10 minutes, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month, then 3 months. Miss it and it drops to the start and comes back within a few cards, again a little later, and more often than the others until it sticks. New cards arrive a few at a time so reviews never pile up. The app also times how long you take to recall each letter. A correct answer after a long pause counts as shaky: it moves up less, comes back sooner, and shows up more, until you can read it fast. Amber dots on the map mark those letters. Come back daily; short sessions beat long ones.
